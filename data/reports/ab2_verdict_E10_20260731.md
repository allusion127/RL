### 2.1 What the numbers say about the mechanism

The four non-`cyclen` point estimates are **±0.0005, −0.0002, +0.0009 and
+0.05 ppm** against levels of 0.110, 0.0216, 0.192 and 27.5. Doubling the
ensemble moved every one of them by well under 1% of the residual it was aimed
at. This is not "a real effect the study was too small to see" — it is a
measured absence of movement at the scale where the acceptance bar needs a 20×
reduction (pre-registration §1). **Averaging ten members instead of five does
not touch this residual.**

### 2.2 The `cyclen` regression, reported honestly

Two true things must be stated together, because the addendum registered both
tests:

* The CI **excludes zero** (`ci_hi = −0.0131`), so by §2.3 clause 2 the axis is
  established worse and the rule says REJECT.
* The observed effect (**0.0778**) sits just **below** its own MDE₈₀
  (**0.0797**), so by §2.7 it is also reported as **underpowered**.

These are not contradictory — the MDE is the effect this design could have
detected at 80% power *a priori*; the CI is the realized inference — but the
combination means this is a **thin, borderline regression, not a collapse**.
Seed reproduction per §2.7, seeds 0–4:

| seed | CI | established worse? |
|---:|---|:--:|
| 0 | [−0.11328, −0.01310] | ✅ |
| 1 | [−0.11328, −0.00261] | ✅ |
| 2 | [−0.11495, −0.00473] | ✅ |
| 3 | [−0.11495, −0.01310] | ✅ |
| 4 | [−0.11328, −0.01310] | ✅ |

It reproduces at every seed, so it is **not seed-sensitive** and §2.7's escalate
branch does not apply — but the upper bound ranges from −0.0026 to −0.0131, so
the exclusion is thin at the top end.

**Robustness of the verdict to this call:** even if the `cyclen` regression were
set aside entirely as a marginal artifact, **condition 1 still fails outright** —
0 axes established a gain against a bar of 2. **The REJECT does not rest on the
borderline call.** That is the whole reason the addendum registered a
two-clause bar rather than a single one.

## 5. What this obliges

The addendum's §2.8 falsification is met. The registered consequence:

> ensemble/variance reduction at this scale is not the lever either … the
> specific thing that is falsified is "the residual is member-to-member variance
> that averaging can cancel"; the live alternative becomes "the residual is
> label noise or missing coverage, which no amount of averaging touches".

Combined with round 1's falsification, **two of the three mechanisms named in
pre-registration §1 are now dead**:

| mechanism (§1) | status |
|---|---|
| loss / label engineering | **falsified** — round 1 (A1, A2, A3) |
| ensemble / variance reduction | **falsified** — this addendum (E10) |
| more labels in the deficient cells | **untested — now the leading candidate** |
| verify-many-then-select | **untested** |

Forbidden by the registered consequences: `--ensemble 20`, larger
`--traj-weight`, larger `--map-peak-topk-weight`, and any sweep of
`--map-peak-topk` / `--traj-anchors`.

Three things a round-3 pre-registration must settle before it can claim to test
anything — the first two carried forward unresolved from round 1:

1. **The §5.1 scoring surface is 100% Dataset P / `master_native`.** It cannot
   test a provenance-conditioned arm (round 1 §2.1 — this is why A2 was
   untestable, not merely unpromoted), and it excludes 70.3% of the mapped
   corpus.
2. **The off-surface signal is now a two-arm pattern.** A3 and E10, mechanisms
   with nothing in common, both improve `f_q`/`f_r` markedly on the excluded
   Dataset-A rows while doing nothing on the frozen cells. That is a hypothesis
   about the *surface*, not about the arms, and it deserves a registered test
   rather than another arm.
3. **If the residual is label noise, no model change can reach the bar.** The
   `flatness_noise_measured_20260726` noise-floor work is the natural next read:
   a bar of ~1e-3 on `node_peak` against a measured 0.021 is only attainable if
   the label itself supports it. Round 3 should establish the floor before
   spending another 4–5 GPU-hours attacking it.
