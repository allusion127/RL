Both are properties of the **frozen** inputs, not of the results. They are
reported here because they change what two of the three arms could possibly have
demonstrated on this surface.

Two consequences, both registered in advance by the structure of §5.1 + §2.4
rather than discovered now:

* **Condition 3 (§5.4) is degenerate this round.** The Dataset-P cell set *is*
  the pooled cell set — 36 of 36, identical. The P-restricted recomputation

  condition 3 carries **no independent information**. Its §5.5 trigger ("the
  pooled gain establishes but the P-restricted gain does not") is unreachable
  here, and A1 is therefore not routed to ESCALATE by it.

  therefore inert on 100% of the decision rows; anything A2 shows here is the

only positive point estimates in the round and both straddle the null —

### A2 — `--cbc-provenance-offset` → **HOLD**

Condition 1 fails: `cbc_max` Δ = −0.058 ppm, CI [−0.688, +0.823], SE 0.441,

axes. Beyond the arithmetic: **the mechanism was inert on 100% of the scored
rows** (§2.1) — every frozen-cell row is `master_native`, the reference group

### A3 — `--map-peak-topk-weight 2.0` → **REJECT**

Condition 2 fails on two enforced axes:

* `M2_flat_tercile_rho_map_cov`: CI hi = **−0.0173** < −ε (0.01) → `violated`
* `M5_cell_rho_cyclen`: CI hi = **−0.0272** < −ε (0.02) → `violated`

Both are *established* harms, not unresolved ones. Condition 1 independently

## 10. What §1 now obliges

The falsification condition is met. Per §1, the registered consequence — **not a
choice being made now** — is:

> the next round must move to a different mechanism (more labels in the
> deficient cells, verify-many-then-select, or an ensemble/variance reduction
> change) — **not** to a bigger version of the same knob.

Concretely, this forbids the obvious next moves: raising `--traj-weight` above
0.3, raising `--map-peak-topk-weight` above 2.0, or sweeping `--map-peak-topk` /
`--traj-anchors` (already descoped in §8.7).

Two items that are **not** falsification consequences but are direct products of
executing the rule, and that a round 3 pre-registration has to resolve before it
can claim to test anything:

1. **The §5.1 scoring surface cannot test a provenance-conditioned arm.** The
   frozen 36-cell holdout is 100% `master_native` = the reference group. Any arm
   whose mechanism is indexed by CBC provenance is inert on it. This is a
   surface/mechanism mismatch in the pre-registration, and it is why A2 is HOLD
   rather than informative.
2. **A1's trajectory supervision was tested only where its labels exist, and
   lost there.** §2.4's coverage limitation predicted the *risk* that a pooled
   gain would not survive P-restriction; what actually happened is stronger and
   simpler — the P cells are the whole surface, and `cyclen` got measurably
   worse on them. The coverage caveat did not shield the result; there is
   nothing left to attribute to missing Dataset-A trajectories.
